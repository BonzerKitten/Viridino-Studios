04 / FAQ

Questions

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

Are your games free-to-play?

No. Our games are premium: you buy them once and that’s it.

What do you make your games with?

Blender for every model and Godot for everything else.

Which platforms are your games on?

It depends on the game. Every game above has buttons for the stores where you can find it.

Can I stream or make videos of your games?

Yes, please do. You can stream, record and share videos of any of our games, monetised or not. A link to the game in your description is always appreciated.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Send us an email with the game, your platform and what happened. The address is just below.

Are you hiring?

Not right now. We are always happy to meet people who make games, though, so feel free to say hello.
